Remote Support

Let Mike connect to your computer and fix it from here. One download, one double-click.

Download Remote SupportWindows 10 and 11 · about 8 MB
Three steps
1Download itClick the red button above. If your browser asks, choose Keep.
2Double-click itOpen NERDS-Remote-Support from your Downloads. When Windows asks "Do you want to allow this app to make changes?", click Yes.
3That's itIt takes about a minute. Mike gets your connection info automatically, and it's also shown on your screen. Leave the computer on and he'll connect.
If Windows shows a blue box
Windows protected your PC

Microsoft Defender SmartScreen prevented an unrecognized app from starting. Running this app might put your PC at risk.

More info
Run anywayDon't run

Click More info, then Run anyway.

Windows shows this for small-business programs that haven't paid Microsoft for a signing certificate. It is not a virus warning. The program downloads the official, signed RustDesk remote support app and connects it to NERDS only.

Good to know

You stay in control. Mike can only connect with the password created on your computer, and you'll see everything he does. When the job is done, run the program again and choose Remove NERDS Remote Support. After that, nobody can connect.

Only use this when you're working with NERDS. We'll never call you out of the blue asking to get into your computer. If anyone does, hang up.
