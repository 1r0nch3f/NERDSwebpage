3D Printing

Custom prints, prototypes, and parts from NERDS PC

If you can design it, describe it, or send me a file, I can print it. I run 3D printers in-house and handle everything from one-off replacement parts to prototype runs to creative projects people bring in just because they think it would be cool.

No minimum orders. No design degree required. You tell me what you need, I figure out the best way to print it, and I give you a price before I start. Same personal service as everything else I do.

What I Print

Replacement Parts

GPU brackets, fan mounts, cable management clips, custom case feet, SSD trays, and anything else your build needs that nobody makes off the shelf.

Creative Projects

Tabletop gaming miniatures, cosplay props, desk organizers, gifts, art pieces, phone stands, you name it. If it fits on a print bed and you can imagine it, I will figure out how to make it.

How It Works

Step 1: Tell Me What You Need

Send me a message with what you are looking for. If you have a 3D file (STL, OBJ, 3MF), send that. If you have a photo, a sketch, or just a description, that works too. I have designed plenty of parts from scratch for people who had nothing but an idea.

Step 2: I Give You a Quote

I look at the design, figure out the best material and print settings, and give you a price. No surprises. If it needs design work, I will tell you what that costs upfront too.

Step 3: I Print It

Once you give the go-ahead, I run the print. Most jobs are done within a few days depending on size and complexity. I will send you a photo before you pick it up so you can see it before you drive over.

Step 4: Pick Up or Ship

Grab it locally or I can ship it to you. If it is not right, I make it right. Simple as that.

Materials

PLA

Great for prototypes, display pieces, and most general-use parts. Wide color selection.

PETG

Stronger than PLA with better heat and chemical resistance. Good for functional parts.

TPU

Flexible material for gaskets, grips, phone cases, bumpers, and anything that needs to bend.

ABS

Tough and heat-resistant. Great for automotive parts, enclosures, and mechanical components.

ASA

UV-resistant version of ABS. Perfect for anything that will live outdoors.

Specialty

Carbon fiber, wood fill, glow-in-the-dark, and more. Ask me what is available for your project.

How Pricing Works

Every print is quoted individually based on what you need. I do not charge by the hour or use some calculator that spits out a number. I look at the actual job and give you a fair price.

Here is what goes into the quote:

Material - type and amount of filament used

Print time - how long the printer runs

Complexity - supports, infill, and post-processing needed

Design work - if I need to create or modify a 3D model for you

Don't Have a 3D File?

That is totally fine. Most people who come to me do not have a ready-to-print file. Here is what I can work with:

You Have a File

Send me your STL, OBJ, or 3MF and I will quote it as-is. If it needs tweaks for printability, I will let you know.

You Have a Photo or Sketch

Take a picture of the part you need or draw it on a napkin. I will model it in CAD and send you a preview before printing.

You Have a Broken Part

Bring me the broken piece. I will measure it, model it, and print a replacement. Even if it is in multiple pieces I can usually figure it out.

You Have an Idea

Describe what you need and what it is for. I will design something that works and print a prototype so you can test it before committing.

Common Questions

How long does a print take?

Depends on size and detail. Small parts can be done same-day. Larger or more complex prints usually take 1-3 days. I will give you a timeline with your quote.

How strong are 3D printed parts?

Stronger than most people expect. With the right material and print settings, 3D printed parts can handle real mechanical loads. I will recommend the right material for your use case.

Can you match a specific color?

I keep a range of colors in stock and can order specific colors for your project. If you need an exact brand color match, I can get close with available filaments or paint the finished part.

What is the biggest thing you can print?

My print bed handles most projects. For larger items, I can print in sections and assemble. Tell me what you need and I will figure out the best approach.

Do you do bulk orders?

Yes. If you need 10 or 100 of something, I will quote it with a volume discount. For really large production runs, I can also help you find a manufacturer and I will handle getting your files production-ready.

Got Something You Need Printed?

Send me a message with what you need. A file, a photo, a sketch, or just a description. I will get back to you with a quote.
