  • AI lead scoring and qualification from web forms, email, and phone inquiries

Customer Service

How We Build It

Phase 1: Department Audit (Week 1-2)

We map every process in the target department. Every form, every handoff, every manual step. We interview the people doing the work and document what a typical day, week, and month looks like.

Phase 2: Automation Design (Week 2-3)

We design the automated workflow end to end. This includes selecting the right AI tools, choosing the automation platform (Zapier, Make.com, or Microsoft Power Automate), and mapping how everything connects to your existing software.

Phase 3: Build and Test (Week 3-6)

We build the automations, configure the AI tools, connect everything, and test it with real data. Your team gets early access to try things out and give feedback before we go live.

Phase 4: Launch and Train (Week 6-8)

Full deployment with hands-on team training. We create documentation specific to your workflows so new hires can get up to speed.

Phase 5: Optimize (60 Days Post-Launch)

We monitor how the system performs, fix anything that is not working right, and fine-tune the automations based on real usage. This is included in the package price.
