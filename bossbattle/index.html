NERDS Boss Battle: a free chat game for your stream

Your viewers join a crew of repair techs and fight PC-problem bosses like the Blue Screen Beast and the Thermal Throttler, right along the bottom of your stream. It runs on its own. No login, no bot, nothing to install. Just one link in OBS.

Live demo with pretend viewers. On your stream it sits on a transparent background over your scene.

1. Get your link

https://www.chattnerds.com/bossbattle/game.html?channel=yourtwitchname

2. Add it to OBS or Streamlabs

  1. In your scene, add a new Browser source.
  2. Paste your link into the URL box.
  3. Set the width to 1920 and the height to 300.
  4. Drag it to the bottom of your scene. On a 1080p canvas it fits as is. On a 4K canvas, scale it to 200%.

That's it. It connects to your chat by itself when the source is showing.

How your viewers play

!crewJoin the crew once. The game remembers them from then on. !crew 1Join as a TECH 1 look (or !crew 2 for TECH 2). !tech 1 / !tech 2 switches later. !crewoutLeave the crew. !summonBroadcaster only: calls in THE NERD to save the crew.

After that it's hands-off. Techs fight on their own, throw USB sticks, network cables and RAM, land random crits, and get BLUE SCREENED when the boss gets them. Ten bosses rotate and level up as your crew beats them. If only a couple of people are in, on-call techs fill the crew so the fight still looks good.

Good to know

  • Free. Use it on your stream as long as you like. Please leave the "Powered by NERDS" credit on screen.
  • Twitch only for now. It reads your public Twitch chat and never posts anything.
  • Test it offline: add &demo=1 to your link to fill the crew with pretend viewers.
  • Common chat bots (Nightbot, StreamElements and similar) are ignored automatically.
