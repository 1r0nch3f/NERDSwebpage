Why Reach Out First

PC component prices move constantly. The explosion in AI has created massive demand for GPUs, memory, and storage, which drives prices up and keeps supply tight across the board. Rather than list prices that go stale overnight, we give you a real quote based on current market pricing when you reach out. That way you know exactly what you are paying before you commit.

How It WorksSimple and straightforward

Tell us what you need and we will get you a current price. If you want help picking the right part for your setup, we are happy to walk through your options. Once you approve the quote, we order the part and let you know when it is ready for pickup or can arrange shipping.
