What Happens During the Assessment

The assessment is a 60 to 90 minute on-site visit. Here is what I am looking at:

Step 1: Technology Walkthrough

I look at every piece of software and hardware your business runs on. What tools are you using? What version? Are they cloud-based or local? Can they talk to each other? This tells me what we have to work with and where the gaps are.

Step 2: Workflow Observation

I watch how your staff actually works. Where are they doing things by hand that could be automated? Where are they copying data between systems? Where are they spending time on repetitive tasks that do not require human judgment?

Step 3: Staff Interviews

I talk to the people doing the work. They know better than anyone what slows them down, what frustrates them, and what falls through the cracks. These conversations usually surface the biggest opportunities.

Step 4: Pain Point Mapping

I map out every bottleneck, repetitive task, and manual process I find. Then I match each one against available AI tools and automation platforms to see what is solvable right now versus what needs more investment.
