Why Local Over Cloud

Local Workstation

  • One-time cost, runs for years
  • Your data never leaves your building
  • No per-hour or per-token charges
  • No waiting on cloud queue times
  • Dedicated to your workloads 24/7
  • Works without internet if needed

Cloud Compute

  • Ongoing monthly costs that add up
  • Data leaves your network
  • Charges by the hour or by the token
  • Queue times during peak demand
  • Shared resources with other users
  • Requires reliable internet connection

For businesses running AI tools daily, a local workstation pays for itself in months. For medical offices, law firms, financial services, or any business handling sensitive information, keeping data on-site is not just convenient. It is a compliance advantage.
